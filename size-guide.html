Size Guide

Most ZOEZONE pieces are cut oversized and relaxed by design. When in doubt, size down for a slightly closer fit.

Tops (Tees, Hoodies, Vests, Blazers)

Measurements in inches, laid flat. Chest is measured pit to pit; length is measured from the shoulder seam.

Bottoms (Denim, Shorts, Skirts, Trousers)

Waist sizes are numeric (inches). Shorts and skirts follow standard S–XL sizing.

How To Measure

  • Chest: measure straight across the chest, one inch below the armpit.
  • Length: measure from the highest point of the shoulder straight down to the hem.
  • Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline.

Still not sure which size to grab? Message us with your usual size in other brands and we'll help you find the right fit.
