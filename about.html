Our Story

Never Basic

High-end, classy streetwear rooted in authentic Haitian heritage — bringing a bold punk-funk edge to a raw, industrial aesthetic.

Where It Started

ZOEZONE was built on a simple idea: streetwear should carry real identity, not just a logo. Every piece in the collection is designed to sit at the intersection of high-end tailoring and raw, industrial texture — oversized silhouettes, distressed finishes, and graphics pulled from Haitian heritage and Taino symbolism.

We're not chasing trends. We're building a wardrobe for people who never follow the crowd — pieces heavy enough to last, and distinct enough that you won't see them on everyone else.

Made In Haiti

Every ZOEZONE piece is cut and sewn in Haiti, using 100% heavyweight cotton built to hold up to daily wear. Producing locally means shorter supply chains, direct relationships with the people making the clothes, and a level of craftsmanship that fast fashion can't match.

It also means every order you place supports Haitian manufacturing directly — something we don't take for granted.

Never Basic

"Never Basic" isn't just a tagline — it's the filter every design goes through before it makes the cut. If it doesn't say something, it doesn't ship. Expect heavyweight cotton, raw hems, oversized fits, and graphics that mean something.
